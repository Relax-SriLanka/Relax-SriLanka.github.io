Popular Excursion Programs

Private and mini-group tours with flawless logistics. Choose the perfect adventure tailored to your vacation rhythm.

Bestseller

Golden Triangle (2 Days)

Perfect immersion into the island's culture. The route includes ascending Sigiriya rock, visiting the Golden Temple in Dambulla, and the ancient capital of Kandy. You will enjoy a traditional dance show and visit the unique Peradeniya Botanical Garden.

Included in the price: overnight stay in a 4-star hotel with a pool, breakfast, all entrance tickets, and the services of a licensed historical guide.

Extreme

Yala Jeep Safari + Galle

Early departure to Sri Lanka's most famous national park. Switch to open safari jeeps and set off in search of unique Ceylon leopards, sloth bears, and massive herds of elephants.

On the way back, we make a stop at the colonial Galle Fort, built by the Dutch in the 17th century, stroll through its narrow streets, and watch the sunset from the fortress wall overlooking the ocean.

Relax

Whales, Turtles & Ella

The ocean safari starts early morning from Mirissa port on a fast and safe catamaran. Get a chance to see the largest mammal on the planet from an arm's reach.

Next, the route goes through the high-altitude town of Ella. Walk across the famous Nine Arch Bridge, hike up Little Adam's Peak, and refresh by the Ravana Falls.

Premium

Nuwara Eliya Tea Plantations

A trip to Little England. Ascend high into the mountains, surrounded by the emerald carpets of the world's best tea plantations. We will visit a historic tea factory built by the British over 100 years ago.

Learn all the secrets of producing genuine Ceylon tea, enjoy a tasting of premium blends, and purchase the finest varieties directly from the conveyor.
